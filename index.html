About Us

Our Story

Inception – Mr.Beans founded in 2002 as Coffee & Sheesha Lounge

Expanded to 9 outlets till 2010 – Indore, Jaipur, CM's Office in Jaipur, Chandigarh, Ludhiana, Ahmedabad, Raipur, Nagpur & Bangalore

From 2012 to 2014 re-structured Mr.Beans concept from Sheesha & Coffee Lounge to Bistro & Cafe format following the Sheesha Ban. Indore, Jaipur & Bangalore are operating in the current Avatar.

The Company is expanding with multiple Outlets in Delhi-NCR region.

Step into the world of Mr. Beans - Coffee & Sheesha Lounge, where cozy vibes meet chic ambiance. Located in the heart of Indore at High Street Apollo, this 360° virtual tour takes you through every corner of our lounge—whether it's the soothing coffee bar, vibrant sheesha spots, or our stylish seating areas. Explore the place that's known for great conversations, perfect brews, and an unforgettable lounge experience, all from the comfort of your screen.
